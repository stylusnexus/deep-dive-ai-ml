Each unchecked item has a lesson that teaches it. The lessons are in the statistics-for-ai course and its math companion.

  • Derivative in an optimisation problem: Math lesson 4, Derivatives
  • Matrix multiplication shapes: Math lesson 2, Matrices
  • Mean versus median: Statistics lesson 2, Describing Data
  • Confidence intervals: Statistics lesson 5, Sampling and Estimation, then lesson 16, Bayesian Inference for how a credible interval differs
  • Leakage from fitting an imputer or scaler before the split: Statistics lesson 17, Study Design and lesson 18, Resampling
