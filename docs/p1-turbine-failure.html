Block 2 exit criterion. The energy project.

Analyse equipment data from a wind energy provider, build models to identify potential failures, and enable timely repairs before equipment breaks down.

Before you write any code

Fill in intake.md. All ten questions, dated, before touching the data. That is not ceremony - question 2 (the dumb baseline) and question 6 (the cost of each error type) both determine how you will read every later result, and neither is answerable once you have seen a score you like.

The honest framing

The interesting output of this project is not the model. It is the sentence "at a threshold of X we catch Y% of failures for Z wasted call-outs, which costs about £N a year against £M for doing nothing." The model is how you get that sentence; the sentence is what anyone will actually use.
