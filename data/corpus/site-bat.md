# Beinn an Tuirc Site Profile

Site code: BAT. Region: West.
Operational turbines: 29.
Site manager: R. Achebe.

## Operating envelope

Turbines at Beinn an Tuirc operate under the standard envelope defined in
OPS-STD-04 unless a site variance is recorded. Gearbox temperature is
logged every fifteen minutes and reported daily to the regional desk.

## Escalation

Faults are escalated to the West regional desk. Out of hours,
escalation passes to the national control room.
