# Whitelee Site Profile

Site code: WHL. Region: Central.
Operational turbines: 68.
Site manager: M. Lindqvist.

## Operating envelope

Turbines at Whitelee operate under the standard envelope defined in
OPS-STD-04 unless a site variance is recorded. Gearbox temperature is
logged every fifteen minutes and reported daily to the regional desk.

## Escalation

Faults are escalated to the Central regional desk. Out of hours,
escalation passes to the national control room.
