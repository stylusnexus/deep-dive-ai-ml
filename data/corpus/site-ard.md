# Ardrossan Site Profile

Site code: ARD. Region: North West.
Operational turbines: 42.
Site manager: J. Okafor.

## Operating envelope

Turbines at Ardrossan operate under the standard envelope defined in
OPS-STD-04 unless a site variance is recorded. Gearbox temperature is
logged every fifteen minutes and reported daily to the regional desk.

## Escalation

Faults are escalated to the North West regional desk. Out of hours,
escalation passes to the national control room.
