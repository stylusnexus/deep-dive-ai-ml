# Clyde Site Profile

Site code: CLY. Region: South.
Operational turbines: 51.
Site manager: S. Tran.

## Operating envelope

Turbines at Clyde operate under the standard envelope defined in
OPS-STD-04 unless a site variance is recorded. Gearbox temperature is
logged every fifteen minutes and reported daily to the regional desk.

## Escalation

Faults are escalated to the South regional desk. Out of hours,
escalation passes to the national control room.
