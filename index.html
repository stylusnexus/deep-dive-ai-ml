  30. Memory, Compaction and Session Decay

  31. Why the Graphics Processing Unit (GPU) Is Waiting

The program

The checklists, exams and records that surround the lessons.

  • Curriculum

    All ten blocks, week by week, each with an exit criterion that runs rather than a box that ticks.

  • Project intake sheet

    Ten questions to answer before writing code. Filled in before every project, reviewed after.

    For choosing a model, a vendor, or a framework — three decisions with three different failure modes.

    The intake sheets, scorecard, exam and rubric as pages you can fill in, then download or print.

    Ten scenarios across Blocks 3 to 8, each needing two or more lessons, with a marking key.

    A scored 45-minute mock interview on the nine system-design questions AI roles keep asking.

    Every compressed, load-bearing line in the course, collected and linked back to the lesson that earns it.

    Aviation-style: killer items marked, hard gates, and a separate non-normal checklist for when something is already wrong.

  • Glossary

    Every term the program defines, accumulating block by block and feeding spaced repetition.
